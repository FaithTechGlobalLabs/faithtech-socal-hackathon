# FaithTech SoCal × Baptize The World Hackathon

**Saturday, October 24, 2026 · The Robertson Triangle, 3390 Robertson Pl, Los Angeles**

Three challenges from Baptize The World, a global movement uniting churches in baptism. On
Pentecost 2026, more than 1,600 churches in 49 countries reported 17,910 baptisms. The next global
day of baptism is Pentecost, May 16, 2027.

This repo has what each challenge needs: a short brief, how Baptize works today, and made-up test
data. For the schedule, judging and everything else about the day, use the participant hub.

## The three tracks

| | Track | How might we... |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [**Mobile-first church onboarding**](tracks/1-mobile-church-onboarding/) (primary focus) | help a pastor anywhere register their church, get resources and report baptisms from the phone they already use, on any connection and in their own language, so every baptism on Pentecost 2027 gets counted? |
| 2 | [**Testimony sharing**](tracks/2-testimony-sharing/) | help someone who was just baptized tell their story in their own words and share it, without anyone at their church having to produce it, so that one baptism invites the next? |
| 3 | [**Post-baptism discipleship**](tracks/3-first-30-days/) | help local churches walk a newly baptized person through their first 30 days of following Jesus, so they keep growing and stay rooted in that church? |

## Reach people where they are

We would love your tool to reach people on WhatsApp, where many of Baptize's churches already
are. It does not have to be built around WhatsApp. A texting agent like [Poke](https://poke.com)
that people can also reach on WhatsApp is the kind of thing we mean. That is a hope, not a rule.
