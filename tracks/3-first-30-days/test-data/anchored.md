# Anchored: structure for Track 3

*Anchored: The Way of Jesus* is an 8-week discipleship journey. This file lays out its structure so
your team can build around it. The full text is not in this repo. Weeks 1 to 3 are below, and more
weeks are coming.

## The arc

**Know Jesus → Become Like Jesus → Live Like Jesus → Multiply Jesus.** Theme verse: Luke 6:40.

| Stage | In one line |
|---|---|
| Know Jesus | Discover who Jesus truly is, and surrender our lives to Him. |
| Become like Jesus | The Holy Spirit transforms our character, desires, thinking, habits, relationships and decisions. |
| Live like Jesus | Love, forgive, serve, give, pray, welcome, obey and bring hope the way Jesus did. |
| Multiply Jesus | Bear witness, share our story, announce the good news and make disciples. |

A disciple is someone who is with Jesus, becoming like Jesus, learning to do what Jesus did, and
helping others do the same, through the power of the Holy Spirit.

## The 8 rhythms

Rhythms for a lifetime of following Jesus, not only for eight weeks.

| # | Rhythm | In one line | Scripture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Surrender daily | Jesus is Lord. Every day we place our yes before Him again. | Luke 9:23 |
| 2 | Abide in the Word | God's Word forms our lives. | Luke 24:27 |
| 3 | Pray and listen | We live in relationship with God. | John 10:27 |
| 4 | Walk in freedom | Jesus leads us into freedom and transformation. | Galatians 5:1 |
| 5 | Live in community | We were never meant to follow Jesus alone. | Ephesians 2:19 |
| 6 | Serve and give | Because Jesus gave Himself for us, we live with open hands. | Mark 10:45 |
| 7 | Share your story | We live sent. | Acts 1:8 |
| 8 | Make disciples | Disciples make disciples. | Matthew 28:19 |

## Every week has the same parts

Rhythm · Key scriptures · Big idea · Teaching · Heart check · Central question · Practice ·
Weekly challenge · Memory verse

## Week 1: Come and follow Jesus

- **Rhythm:** Surrender daily
- **Key scriptures:** Mark 1:14–20; Luke 9:23–25; John 1:14–18; John 14:6–11; Romans 5:6–11;
  Romans 6:4–14; Romans 8:14–17; Romans 10:9; 2 Corinthians 5:14–17; Ephesians 2:8–10;
  Colossians 1:13–20
- **Big idea:** The gospel is the good news that Jesus has come, died, risen, defeated sin and
  death, and now reigns as Lord. Following Jesus begins with trusting the goodness of God and
  bringing our whole lives under His loving leadership.
- **Teaching topics:** Who do you believe God is? · Jesus shows us the Father · The gospel is good
  news · The cross shows us God's love · Jesus has won · You have been rescued · New life in Christ,
  with baptism as a picture of the gospel · You are a son or daughter · Repent and believe · Come
  and follow me · Jesus is Savior and Lord · Have you said yes to Jesus? (repent, believe, receive,
  surrender) · If you said yes today · How can I know I am saved? · Two questions every disciple
  must answer
- **Central question:** When Jesus' way and my desires disagree, who gets the final word?
- **Practice:** Begin each morning with open hands. Pray a short prayer of surrender slowly, then
  ask: "Jesus, is there anything You want me to surrender today?"
- **Weekly challenge:** Name one area you have resisted surrendering to Jesus, and take one
  concrete step of obedience this week.
- **Memory verse:** 2 Corinthians 5:17
- **If you said yes today:** tell your group leader or another trusted Christian. The next step is
  baptism.

## Week 2: Know God and abide in His Word

- **Rhythm:** Abide in the Word
- **Key scriptures:** Luke 24:25–32, 44–49; John 1:1–18; John 5:39–40; John 15:1–11;
  2 Timothy 3:14–17; Hebrews 1:1–3; Colossians 3:16–17
- **Big idea:** Scripture tells the story that finds its center and fulfillment in Jesus. As we
  abide in His Word, that story begins to reshape our lives.
- **Teaching topics:** Jesus at the center · Reading Scripture through Jesus · The way of Jesus
- **Central question:** What has Jesus already shown me that I have not yet put into practice?
- **Practice:** Read (what does the passage say?) · Story (where does it fit in God's larger
  story?) · Jesus (what does it reveal about Jesus and His Kingdom?) · Listen (Holy Spirit, what
  are You showing me?) · Respond (what will I do because this is true?)
- **Weekly challenge:** At least 20 uninterrupted minutes each day in one Gospel. Watch Jesus, and
  practice one thing you see.
- **Memory verse:** John 15:5

## Week 3: Prayer and walking with the Holy Spirit

- **Rhythm:** Pray and listen
- **Key scriptures:** Luke 11:1–13; Matthew 6:5–15; John 14:15–27; John 16:7–15;
  Romans 8:1–17, 26–27; Galatians 5:16–25; Acts 1:4–8; Acts 2:1–4
- **Big idea:** The Christian life is not our attempt to imitate Jesus through human strength. It
  is the life of Jesus being formed in us through the Holy Spirit.
- **Teaching topics:** The gift of the Spirit · Keep in step · The secret place: bless His heart
  (worship), share my heart (be honest), receive His heart (delight in Him), pray His heart (agree
  with Him)
- **Central question:** Am I trying to live for God, or am I learning to live with God?
- **Practice:** Three pauses each day: morning, midday and evening.
- **Weekly challenge:** Protect one extended period this week for unhurried prayer and listening.
- **Memory verse:** Galatians 5:25

## The "when" cues so far

| Cadence | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Every morning | Week 1 practice: begin each morning with open hands |
| 20 minutes a day | Week 2 challenge: time in one Gospel |
| Three times a day | Week 3 practice: morning, midday and evening pauses |
| Once a week | Each week's challenge and memory verse |
| Right away | Week 1: tell your group leader or another trusted Christian |
