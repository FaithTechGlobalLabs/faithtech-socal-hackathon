# A simple first-30-days plan (lighter fallback)

**Anchored is the main content for this track: see [anchored.md](anchored.md).** This plan is a
lighter option if your team wants one.

baptize.org's Next Steps page already points new believers to three things: find a church, read the
Gospel of John, share your story. This outline builds on that.

| Days | Reading | One small next step (example) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | John 1 | Tell one person you got baptized |
| 2 to 21 | John 2 to John 21, one chapter a day | One question to reflect on, one line to reply with |
| 7 | (with John 7) | Go to a service or small group at your church this week |
| 14 | (with John 14) | Ask someone at church to pray with you |
| 21 | (with John 21) | Share your story (link to Track 2) |
| 22 to 30 | Your church's choice | Left open for the local church to fill in |

Every message needs an opt-in and an easy way to stop.
