# Track 2: Testimony sharing
**How might we help someone who was just baptized tell their story in their own words and share it, without anyone at their church having to produce it, so that one baptism invites the next?**

**Short version:** How might we make it easy for someone who was just baptized to share their story?

**Who it's for:** design, frontend, video and media, storytellers

Every baptism is a story worth telling. Today a shareable testimony depends on someone at a church producing it, and a story only reaches baptize.org if someone on the Baptize team enters it by hand. The site's Next Steps page tells people "Your baptism is a testimony" and links to a generic invite page. The movement needs these stories, in every language, to invite the next church and the next person ahead of Pentecost 2027.

**Angles a team can take**
- How might we make telling your story as easy as answering a few questions, in any language?
- How might we turn a raw story into something a person actually wants to post?
- How might we get the best stories to the Baptize team, with permission, so the movement can tell them too?

**A strong demo looks like:** someone who was baptized opens a link, answers a few guided prompts (typed, spoken or a short video), and leaves with a testimony they can post right away, without anyone at a church touching it. If they say yes, the Baptize team can see the story and feature it.

**Must also:** nothing goes public without the person's OK, and anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian's OK.

---

## How Baptize does it today

- Testimonies are stories the Baptize team adds and features by hand (quote, name, church, photo or video).
- **A newly baptized person has no way to share their own story yet.** The "Share your story" card on the next-steps page is a placeholder.
- **There is no Baptize API to call.** Mock the save step and use the test data. Details: [How Baptize works today](../../docs/baptize-today.md).

## Test data

- [`test-data/testimony-prompts.md`](test-data/testimony-prompts.md): a starter set of guided prompts to adapt, translate or replace.
- [`test-data/sample-stories.md`](test-data/sample-stories.md): three made-up raw testimonies (rambling, short, non-English) to turn into something shareable.
