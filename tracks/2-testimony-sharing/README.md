# Track 2: Testimony sharing

> How might we help someone who was just baptized tell their story in their own words and share
> it, without anyone at their church having to produce it, so that one baptism invites the next?

**Who it's for.** Someone who was just baptized, and the next person their story might reach.

**Today.** A shareable testimony depends on someone at a church producing it, and a story only
reaches baptize.org if someone on the Baptize team enters it by hand. The "Share your story" card
on the site's Next Steps page is a placeholder.

**If you crack it.** One baptism invites the next. The movement gets these stories, in every
language, to invite the next church and the next person ahead of Pentecost 2027.

**Reach people where they are.** We would love your tool to reach people on WhatsApp, where many
of Baptize's churches already are. It does not have to be built around WhatsApp. A texting agent
like [Poke](https://poke.com) that people can also reach on WhatsApp is the kind of thing we mean.
That is a hope, not a rule. Tools that help: [text-agent kit](../../docs/text-agent-kit.md).

## Why it's hard

- Telling your story should be as easy as answering a few questions, typed, spoken or on video,
  in any language.
- A raw story has to become something a person actually wants to post.
- The best stories should reach the Baptize team, with permission, so the movement can tell them
  too.

## Guardrail

Nothing goes public without the person's OK. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian's OK.
