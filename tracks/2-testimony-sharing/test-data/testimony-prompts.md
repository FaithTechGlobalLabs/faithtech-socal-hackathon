# Starter testimony prompts

A starting point. Shorten, translate, reorder or replace them. Keep it to a few minutes.

1. What's your first name, or the name you'd like on your story?
2. Before you were baptized, what was life like? One or two sentences.
3. What made you decide to get baptized?
4. What was the moment like, coming up out of the water?
5. What would you say to someone who is thinking about it?
6. Is there a verse that means something to you right now? (Optional.)
7. Can we share your story? Only with you / With my church / With Baptize The World too.
8. Are you 18 or older? If not, a parent or guardian needs to say yes before anything is shared.
