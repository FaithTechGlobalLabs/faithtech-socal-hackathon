# Ground rules

Baptize The World serves pastors and new believers in Peru, Brazil, Asia and beyond. These rules
protect them, the movement's numbers and you.

## 1. Test data only

- No real church, pastor, phone number, email or baptism record. From anyone. For any reason.
- Every file in `tracks/*/test-data/` is made up. Use it, or make up more like it.
- Use your own phone or a teammate's to test messaging. Never text a number you found in a file.

## 2. Prototypes only

- Nothing you build today connects to, reads from or writes to baptize.org or its systems.
- Nothing sends real messages to real people you do not know. Test with your own team.
- If Baptize The World wants to take a prototype further, that is a conversation after today.

## 3. Consent first

- **Messaging:** get an opt-in before sending anything, and make "STOP" work.
- **Stories:** nothing goes public without the person's OK.
- **Under 18:** a parent or guardian's OK is required before a story is shared.

## 4. Numbers have to be trustworthy

The movement reports on these counts. A flow that accepts anything is worse than the form it
replaces. Reject duplicates, non-numbers and impossible counts, and say why.

## 5. Do not invent facts

- No made-up Scripture references or quotes. If you quote the Bible, cite book, chapter and verse.
- No made-up church addresses or service times.
- When the source does not say, your tool says it does not know.

## 6. Build in quality control

AI makes mistakes, including in translation. Build a way to catch them. How is up to you.

## 7. Start fresh, credit sources

Nothing built before 12:30 PM counts. Credit libraries, templates and other people's code.
