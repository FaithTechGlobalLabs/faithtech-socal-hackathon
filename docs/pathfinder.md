# Pathfinder

Pathfinder is a lightweight tool from FaithTech, built by Kevin, that your team uses during the
day.

## What it does

- Your team signs up by scanning a **QR code**.
- You enter your **laments** and carry them through the day.
- It tracks how many **builders** came, how many **projects started** and how many were
  **deployed**.

## How to join

After your team forms and picks a track, scan the QR code on the screen. The Pathfinder link is
**posted here at 9:00 AM.**

## Submitting your code

Pathfinder is for your team's journey, not for turning in code. **Code-freeze submissions go
through the Submit form on the hub** (<https://faithtech-socal-hackathon.vercel.app/#submit>)
unless it is announced otherwise on the day. See [submission](submission.md).
