# Pathfinder

Pathfinder is FaithTech's Create program app. Your team works through every step of the Create
Cycle in it: Lament, Discern, Build and Steward.

**[Open Pathfinder](https://pathfinder.faithtech.com/new-experience)**

## When

Project leads set it up for the team at **10:25 AM**, right after teams form.

## How to join

1. Sign in, or sign up if you're new.
2. Add your details: name and role.
3. Choose your experience.
4. Pick your event or cohort, if it's listed.
5. Set up your team: team name, your track's challenge, and your members.

## Submitting your project

Pathfinder is for your team's journey, not for turning in code. Submit through the
[submission form](https://faithtechhub.typeform.com/to/nIdH7FbF) by 4:00 PM. See
[submission](submission.md).
