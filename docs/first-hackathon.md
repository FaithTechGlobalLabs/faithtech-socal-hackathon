# Your first hackathon

What to expect, in two minutes.

## You do not need

- **A team.** Teams of 3 to 5 form around a track at 10:05. Mix builders, designers and church voices. Come alone; most people do.
- **An idea.** Baptize The World brings the problems. You pick one with your team.
- **To write code.** AI writes code from plain descriptions. Teams also need people who understand
  churches and pastors, test the tool, write the words on screen, pray and run the demo.
- **Anything prepared.** The code starts today.

## Every team has

- **A Project Lead** who keeps the team pointed at one thing and sets up Pathfinder.
- **A Dev** who owns the repo. Vibe-coding is okay.
- **A role for everyone else.** Mentors will help you find one.

## What usually works

- **Small and working beats big and unfinished.**
- **Name your direction before lunch.** Then build.
- **Push to GitHub every hour or so.**
- **Pray, prototype, pause.** Stop and check in as a team more than once.

## Normal things that happen

- Your first idea changes. That is fine.
- Something breaks an hour before code freeze. Demo what works and say what does not.
- You get stuck on setup. Ask a mentor. Nobody should lose the afternoon to an install.

## Demo

3 minutes, then 2 minutes of questions, from 4:15. Start with the person you built it for, show it working live (on a phone if you can), name the fruit and the risks, then tell us what God did in your team. Have a backup video ready: [how to make one](demo-video.md).
This is a demo, not a pitch. We already know the problem.
