Pick one track

Teams of 3 to 5. Each team has a project lead and a primary developer, and a role for everyone.

01 · top priority

Mobile-first church onboarding

How might we make registering a church and reporting baptisms as easy as sending a text?

Done at 6 PM: a judge texts a number from their own phone, registers a church in their language, and sees it on a live dashboard. Then texts a baptism count and the total moves.

02

Testimony sharing

How might we make it easy for someone who was just baptized to share their story?

Done at 6 PM: someone opens a link, answers a few guided prompts, and leaves with a testimony they can post, without anyone at a church producing it.

03 · stretch

The first 30 days

How might we walk with someone through their first 30 days after baptism?

Done at 6 PM: a judge enrolls by text or QR code, gets Day 1, replies, gets the next step. Their church can see who is active and who has gone quiet.

Build rules

  1. Start fresh today. Any stack.
  2. Test data only. No real church, pastor or personal data. Use the files in the challenge repo.
  3. Consent first. Opt-in before any message; nothing public without the person's OK; under 18 needs a parent's OK.
  4. Credit your sources.
  5. Code freeze at 4:00 PM. Push to a public GitHub repo and submit the link.

Submit and demo

  • Submit by 4:00 PM: team name, track, public repo link, one-paragraph write-up. TBD: form
  • Optional: a short video walkthrough. Ask Claude to click through your prototype and record it.
  • Demo: 90 seconds of working prototype, then tell us how your team experienced the day and what God did. A demo, not a pitch.
